# 42next

42next is the **internal admin** single-page application, used by the Mixpla team. It talks to
datanest, where row-level security keeps every query user-scoped.

It is not a listener app and not where a station is created or operated. Owners work in Mixdeck, and
listeners just open a station's public link — no app and no account. Never describe 42next as something
a listener or a station owner uses.
