# The volume problem

AI music generation has exploded, and Suno alone reports on the order of 100 million users and around
300 million dollars of annual revenue. The signal that matters is that the cost of creating music has
collapsed: independent producers and AI tools are generating very large volumes of content.

So the bottleneck has moved. Creation is solved; **intelligent distribution and curation** is not, and
nobody has built infrastructure for it. That gap is what Mixpla occupies.

# Why the incumbents can't take this

The large streaming services are constrained rather than slow. Spotify is committed to
billion-dollar label agreements and cannot promote independent or AI-generated music without putting
those relationships at risk. Mixpla carries no catalogue obligations, so it is free to follow the music.

RadioKing and AzuraCast are the closest existing tools and do their job well, but what they sell is
infrastructure — a virtual studio for a human operator. When the human steps away, the air goes static.
Mixpla sits above that layer rather than competing with it.
